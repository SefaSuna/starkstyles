Privacy Policy — Stark Styles

Last Updated: October 29, 2026

Stark Styles operates an Etsy shop at https://www.etsy.com/shop/StarkStyles. This policy describes the intended privacy practices for an application used to share Stark Styles product listings on Pinterest.

1. Purpose

This application is intended to help manage and share Stark Styles product listings on Pinterest.

2. Information We Access

When authorized, the application may access Pinterest account information and permissions required to create or manage Pins. Access is limited to the functionality necessary for the intended purpose.

3. Use of Information

Information accessed through the Pinterest API is used only to provide the requested Pinterest integration. We do not sell personal information or share Pinterest API data with third parties for advertising purposes.

4. Data Storage and Security

We take reasonable measures to protect application credentials and any information processed through the integration. Pinterest API data is not stored except where explicitly permitted by applicable Pinterest policies.

5. Third-Party Services

This application may interact with Pinterest and Etsy. Their respective privacy policies govern their services.

  • Pinterest Privacy Policy
  • Etsy Privacy Policy

6. Data Deletion and Contact

For questions about this privacy policy or requests concerning information handled by this application, please contact the Stark Styles shop through Etsy.

7. Changes

This policy may be updated when the application's functionality or applicable requirements change.
